## **3. Company Analysis: AMZN**

### **A. The OpenAI Strategic Alliance**
Amazon and OpenAI have announced a landmark multi-billion dollar partnership. Amazon is investing **$50 billion** in OpenAI, while OpenAI has committed to spending **$138 billion on AWS cloud services** over the next eight years. This deal effectively secures a massive, long-term revenue stream for AWS and cements its position as the preferred infrastructure for the world's leading AI research organization.

### **B. Project Kuiper & Delta Air Lines Deal**
Amazon’s Leo satellite internet unit (Project Kuiper) has landed a flagship contract with **Delta Air Lines**. Starting in 2028, Amazon will provide in-flight Wi-Fi for 500 aircraft, directly challenging SpaceX’s Starlink. This validates Amazon's $10 billion space investment and opens a new high-margin service revenue vertical.

### **C. Financial Services Pivot**
In a significant shift for its fintech ecosystem, Amazon is moving its small-business credit card programs from American Express to **U.S. Bank and Mastercard**. This transition is expected to optimize its payment infrastructure and potentially improve terms for its massive seller base.

### **D. Regulatory Concessions**
To mitigate antitrust pressure in the UK, Amazon and Microsoft have agreed to demands regarding interoperability and the elimination of certain data transfer fees. While this increases competition, it reduces the immediate risk of more severe regulatory "break-up" scenarios or massive fines.
