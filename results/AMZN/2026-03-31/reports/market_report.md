#### 1. Executive Summary
**AMZN** is currently exhibiting a strong **bearish trend** across all timeframes. The stock closed at **$200.95** on March 30, significantly below its major moving averages. Momentum indicators suggest that the downward pressure is accelerating, although the RSI is approaching levels that might eventually invite a relief bounce. Traders should exercise caution as the stock is in a "sell the rallies" environment.

This indicates that the average price paid by investors, weighted by volume over the recent period, is higher than the current market price, suggesting that recent sellers are in control and buyers are not stepping in with conviction.

#### 5. Actionable Insights and Strategy
*   **For Bearish Traders:** The primary strategy remains "selling the rip." Any rally toward the **10-day EMA ($206.45)** or the **Bollinger Middle ($210.16)** should be viewed as a potential entry point for short positions, provided bearish momentum remains intact on smaller timeframes.
